About me

For ten years, with Huawei, Sofrecom (Orange) and inwi, my job was to keep mobile networks healthy. That meant reading thousands of performance counters every day, spotting what went wrong, proving why, and measuring whether a fix actually worked.

That is data analysis. Now I'm bringing the same habits (clean data, clear KPIs, root causes over guesses) to organizations in Nova Scotia that need their data to make sense to the people using it.
